# References

## Books
1. A. Silberschatz, P. B. Galvin, G. Gagne, *Operating System Concepts*, 10th ed., Wiley, 2018 - chapters on process synchronization (semaphores, monitors, readers-writers, dining philosophers).
2. A. S. Tanenbaum, H. Bos, *Modern Operating Systems*, 4th ed., Pearson, 2014 - inter-process communication and classical IPC problems.

## Original papers
3. E. W. Dijkstra, "Hierarchical ordering of sequential processes", *Acta Informatica* 1(2), 115-138, 1971 - the dining philosophers problem.
4. P. J. Courtois, F. Heymans, D. L. Parnas, "Concurrent control with 'readers' and 'writers'", *Communications of the ACM* 14(10), 667-668, 1971 - the readers-writers problem.
5. C. A. R. Hoare, "Monitors: an operating system structuring concept", *Communications of the ACM* 17(10), 549-557, 1974.

## Documentation

7. Oracle, *The Java Tutorials* - "Concurrency: Synchronized Methods" and "Guarded Blocks" (wait / notifyAll).
